# LAST SPARK ⚡

**One robot. One dying battery. One last chance to restore the power.**

LAST SPARK is a short, browser-playable platformer made in **GDevelop** for **GDevelop BIG Game Jam #10**, themed *Keep Me Alive*.

Play as a small maintenance robot exploring an abandoned industrial power station. Collect energy to stay operational, cross increasingly challenging platforms, and reach the emergency generator before your battery runs out.

**Release:** Game Jam Submission, November 2026  
**Developer:** Solo project  
**Engine:** GDevelop  
**Platform:** Web browser  
**Resolution:** 960 × 540

|---|---|

| Space | Jump |
| E | Activate the generator |
| R | Restart after Game Over |

## Gameplay

Your robot begins with **50% battery**. Energy steadily drains as you explore the facility.

Collect energy pickups to restore 20% battery, up to a maximum of 100%.

Reach the emergency generator before your battery reaches zero to restore power and win.

## Features

- Handcrafted industrial platforming level
- Battery-management survival mechanic
- Energy pickups and challenging platform gaps
- Atmospheric power-station environment
- Victory, Game Over, and retry systems
- Free browser-based gameplay

## Development

LAST SPARK was developed as a solo game jam project with AI-assisted development.

GDevelop AI assisted with event implementation and troubleshooting, while ChatGPT assisted with planning, debugging, custom artwork, and animation alignment.

The project's gameplay design, playtesting, creative decisions, and release process were directed by its developer.
